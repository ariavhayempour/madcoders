// mission

Building software and community at UW–Madison.

MadCoders is a student organization dedicated to software engineering, open-source collaboration, and technology skills — whether you're writing your first lines of code or shipping full-stack applications. Our weekly meetings cover tutorials, project collaboration, interview prep, and tech discussions.

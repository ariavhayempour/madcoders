📄 README.md

Building software and community at UW–Madison.

MadCoders is a student organization dedicated to software engineering, open-source collaboration, and technology skills — whether you're writing your first lines of code or shipping full-stack applications.

Our weekly meetings cover coding tutorials, project collaboration, interview prep, and tech discussions designed to help every member build confidence as a developer.
